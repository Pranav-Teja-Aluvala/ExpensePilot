Privacy at a glance

ExpensePilot is designed so your financial data never leaves your browser.

What we access

With your explicit consent, Gmail read-only access is used to scan messages only from supported banks (HDFC, ICICI, SBI, Axis, Kotak, Union Bank).

What we store — locally, in your browser

  • Transaction date
  • Amount and type (Credit/Debit)
  • Merchant name (if present)
  • Bank name
  • Available balance (if present)

What we never do

  • Store the original email body or subject
  • Upload data to any server
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use analytics or trackers

Your controls

Export or delete all data at any time from Settings. Disconnecting Gmail revokes the token and stops all scans.
